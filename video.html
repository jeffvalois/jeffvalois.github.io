DCU · Original Series

Starting 9

Ahead of the Worcester Red Sox inaugural season in 2021, DCU became a founding sponsor of Polar Park. To introduce the partnership, I wrote and produced DCU’s Starting 9—nine short segments filmed at Polar Park, each highlighting a little-known piece of Worcester baseball history.

For nine days leading up to Opening Day on May 11, a new story was released each day.

Role: Concept · Writing · Producing
