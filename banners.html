Sports history / print design

Banners

Banners is a series of sports prints built around the visual language of championship banners—using repetition, typography, and team history to turn decades of success into a single graphic statement.

The first piece brings all 18 Boston Celtics championship seasons together in one composition, creating a pattern that works both as a historical record and as a bold piece of sports design.

Role: Concept, Illustration & Design
