About
Creative storytelling is my jam.

Whether it’s through design, photography, videography, or copywriting, I love to create things that tell a story and convey a message. Personally and professionally, creating is a vital outlet for me.

Over 15+ years in the marketing world, I’ve experienced the work from a lot of different roles—from Marketing Specialist to Senior Designer to Brand Strategy Manager and everything in between. I’ve learned a ton from each role and from the people on those teams.

At home, I’m a lucky husband to Sarah and a dad to Jackson and our dog Lyla. When the weather’s nice (and sometimes when it’s not), you’ll probably find me on the golf course.
